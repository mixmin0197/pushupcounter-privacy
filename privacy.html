摄像头Camera

只有在你开始俯卧撑或平板支撑训练后，App 才会打开 iPhone 的前置 TrueDepth 摄像头，并且只读取深度数据。每一帧在手机上实时算出距离和角度后立即丢弃。App 不拍照、不录像，不保存也不上传任何图像或深度数据。训练结束后摄像头随即关闭。

The app turns on the iPhone's front TrueDepth camera only after you start a push-up or plank workout, and it reads only depth data. Each frame is turned into a distance and an angle on the phone and then discarded. The app takes no photos or videos and never stores or uploads images or depth data. The camera turns off when the workout ends.

运动传感器Motion Sensors

  • iPhone 的运动数据用于训练前的水平仪，帮你把手机放平。
  • AirPods（Pro、第 3 代及以后、Max）的头部运动数据用于识别仰卧起坐。
  • Apple Watch 的运动数据用于识别深蹲、开合跳，以及在不用手机时计数俯卧撑、计时平板支撑。
  • iPhone motion data powers the level that helps you lay the phone flat before a workout.
  • Head motion from AirPods (Pro, 3rd generation or later, Max) is used to recognize sit-ups.
  • Apple Watch motion data recognizes squats and jumping jacks, and counts push-ups and times planks when you're not using the phone.

这些原始传感器数据只在训练过程中实时使用，不会被保存。App 只记录结果，比如个数、用时和哪些动作不标准。

Raw sensor data is used only in real time during a workout and is not stored. The app keeps only the results, such as rep counts, duration and which reps had form issues.

健康 AppApple Health

经你在系统弹窗中授权后，App 会把训练写入健康 App，包括训练类型、时长、心率和活动能量；会读取训练期间的心率和活动能量，用来显示实时心率和消耗；只有在你点击「从健康 App 读取体重」时才会读取体重。

With your permission in the system prompt, the app saves workouts to Health, including workout type, duration, heart rate and active energy. It reads heart rate and active energy during workouts to show live heart rate and calories, and reads your weight only when you tap "Read Weight from Health".

健康数据不会离开你的设备，不会用于广告或营销，也不会出售或提供给任何第三方。你可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置 › 健康 › 数据访问与设备」里更改授权。

Health data never leaves your devices, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared with any third party. You can change these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间的同步Sync Between iPhone and Apple Watch

训练记录、每日目标、体重、深蹲校准结果和语言设置，通过 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 在你的 iPhone 和与之配对的 Apple Watch 之间直接传输，不经过开发者或任何第三方的服务器。

Workout history, daily goals, weight, squat calibration and language settings are transferred directly between your iPhone and its paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. They never pass through the developer's or any third party's servers.

小组件Widgets

桌面小组件和表盘小组件会显示今天的目标进度、最近 7 天的训练量和消耗的卡路里。这些摘要保存在设备上 App 与小组件共用的存储空间里，不会发送到其他地方。小组件出现在锁屏或表盘上时，旁人可能看到这些数字。

Home Screen and watch face widgets show today's goal progress, the last 7 days of activity and calories burned. This summary is kept in storage shared by the app and its widgets on your device and is not sent anywhere. If you place a widget on your Lock Screen or watch face, people nearby may see these numbers.

导出与删除Export and Deletion

  • 在 App 的「设置 › 导出历史」可以把全部训练记录导出为 CSV 表格。文件由你自己决定分享到哪里。
  • 在「设置 › 清除所有记录」可以一次删除 iPhone 和手表上的全部记录，删除后无法恢复。
  • 删除 App 会同时删除它在设备上保存的所有数据。
  • 已经写入健康 App 的训练不受上述操作影响，需要在健康 App 中删除。
  • In Settings › Export History you can export all workouts as a CSV file. You decide where that file goes.
  • In Settings › Delete All History you can delete every record on both iPhone and Watch at once. This can't be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your devices.
  • Workouts already saved to Apple Health are not affected by any of the above and must be deleted in the Health app.

权限Permissions

摄像头、运动与健身、健康数据都需要你在系统弹窗中同意后才会使用。你可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置」里关闭任意一项；关闭后，依赖它的训练功能将无法使用，其他功能不受影响。

The camera, Motion & Fitness and Health data are used only after you allow them in the system prompt. You can turn any of them off at any time in iPhone Settings; features that depend on that permission will stop working, and everything else keeps working.
